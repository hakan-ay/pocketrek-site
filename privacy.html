Privacy policy

Short version: Pocketrek has no account and no server of its own. Your progress and your Apple Health data stay on your iPhone. We only receive anonymous usage counts, and never anything from Health.

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Applies to the Pocketrek app for iPhone, made by an independent developer.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your progress: your pets, their names, levels and outfits, your garden, the road you travel, postcards, stamps, focus sessions and goals. This is saved inside the app on your iPhone and shared only with Pocketrek's own widgets on the same phone.
  • Apple Health data: if you allow it, Pocketrek reads your step count, workouts and walking, running, cycling and swimming distances. It uses them only to reward your pet and water your garden. Health data is not sent to us or to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not sold.
  • Photos: when you add a photo to a sticker, you pick it yourself with Apple's photo picker, and it is used only on your phone. Pocketrek can save a sticker to your photo library if you ask it to.
  • Reminders: daily reminders and focus notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification service of ours is involved.

What we receive: anonymous usage counts

To learn which parts of Pocketrek help people and which confuse them, the app sends anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. Examples: a focus session finished (and how many minutes), the shop was opened, a postcard was earned, a tree was planted, the subscription screen was shown.

  • Events carry no name, email, contact or advertising identifier. TelemetryDeck receives a one-way, salted code made from an app-specific identifier, so we can count people without knowing who they are.
  • TelemetryDeck also receives basic technical details such as the app version, iOS version, device model and language.
  • Events never contain Apple Health data, step or workout numbers, or anything you typed, such as pet names or session tags.
  • We use these counts only to improve Pocketrek. We do not sell them, share them for advertising, or combine them with other data to identify you.

Purchases

Pocketrek Premium is a subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app receives only whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock Premium. You can manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

What we never do

  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no advertising.
  • No location tracking. The map shows the cities on your route, not where you are.
  • No selling or renting of any data.

Your choices

  • You can turn Health access on or off at any time in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › Pocketrek.
  • Deleting Pocketrek from your iPhone deletes everything it stored on the phone.
  • Because usage events are anonymous, we cannot connect them to you. If you want to ask about them anyway, or about anything in this policy, write to hakanay.ita@gmail.com. If you live in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Pocketrek is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If Pocketrek starts doing something new with data, for example adding friends or online backup, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
