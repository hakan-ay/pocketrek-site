My steps or workouts don't show up

Open the Health app › Sharing › Apps › Pocketrek and make sure Steps, Workouts and the distances are turned on. If your steps come from another device or app, such as a watch or a fitness band, they appear once that device has synced them into Health; some bands sync only a few times a day, so the number can jump later.

Pocketrek shows a different step count than Health

Pocketrek reads the same daily total that the Health app shows. If the two differ for a moment, a device is probably still syncing; the numbers catch up together.

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open the Premium screen in Pocketrek and tap Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Pocketrek. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. Items you bought with seeds stay yours.

I don't get my daily reminder

Check Settings › Notifications › Pocketrek, and that Focus modes aren't hiding it. In Pocketrek, the reminder card shows when the next reminder is due and why it may be skipped today.

Will I lose my pet if I change phones?

For now, Pocketrek keeps your progress on your iPhone only. Moving it to a new phone through an iPhone backup or Quick Start keeps it; deleting the app removes it.
