Your pet grows when you do.

Pocketrek is a small companion for focus sessions, workouts and steps. Every goal you reach rains on your garden and moves your pet down a real road, from Verona to Venice and beyond, collecting postcards on the way.

Pocketrek runs on your iPhone. It has no account, and your Apple Health data never leaves your phone.
